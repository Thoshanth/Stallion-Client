FAQs

What Are Your Gym Hours?

Stallion Xtreme Fitness is open Mon-Sat: 5am - 10pm, allowing you to train whenever it fits your schedule.

How Do I Access The Facility?

Access to the gym is simple and secure. You can either scan your phone's bar-code or enter your personal PIN to enter the facility.

How Do I Purchase A Membership?

Memberships can be conveniently claimed through our online platform. You can also scan the QR code located on our door to directly access the membership signup page, making it easy to join anytime.
